Complete Syllabus

What's Inside the Notes?

MSBTE ER20-11T Pharmaceutics syllabus ke exact chapters aur topic coverage ko proper structured format me yahan dikhaya gaya hai.

1

Introduction to Pharmacy & Pharmacopoeia

Chapter 1

  • ▸ History of Pharmacy profession in India with relation to education, industry, practice, and professional associations
  • ▸ Pharmacy as a career
  • ▸ Pharmacopoeia: Introduction to IP, BP, USP, NF, and Extra Pharmacopoeia
  • ▸ Salient features of Indian Pharmacopoeia
2

Packaging Materials

Chapter 2

  • ▸ Types and selection criteria of packaging materials
  • ▸ Advantages and disadvantages of glass, plastic, metal, and rubber as packaging materials
3

Pharmaceutical Aids & Preservatives

Chapter 3

  • ▸ Organoleptic agents: colouring, flavouring, and sweetening agents
  • ▸ Preservatives: definition, types, examples, and uses
4

Unit Operations

Chapter 4

  • ▸ Definition, objectives, applications, principles, construction, and working of unit operations
  • ▸ Size reduction: hammer mill and ball mill
  • ▸ Size separation: classification of powders according to IP, cyclone separator, sieves, and standards of sieves
  • ▸ Mixing: double cone blender, turbine mixer, triple roller mill, and Silverson mixer homogenizer
  • ▸ Filtration: theory of filtration, membrane filter, and sintered glass filter
  • ▸ Drying: fluidized bed dryer and process of freeze drying
  • ▸ Extraction: definition, classification, methods, and applications
5

Dosage Forms & Preparations

Chapter 5

Core Formulations
Tablets — coated and uncoated, various modified tablets (sustained release, extended-release, fast dissolving, multi-layered, etc.)
Capsules — hard and soft gelatin capsules
Liquid Oral Preparations — solution, syrup, elixir, emulsion, suspension, dry powder for reconstitution
Topical Preparations — ointments, creams, pastes, gels, liniments, lotions, suppositories, and pessaries
Nasal & Ear Preparations — nasal preparations and ear preparations
Powders & Granules — insufflations, dusting powders, effervescent powders, and effervescent granules
Sterile Formulations — injectables, eye drops, and eye ointments
Immunological Products — sera, vaccines, toxoids, and their manufacturing methods
6

Manufacturing Plants, QC & QA

Chapter 6

  • ▸ Basic structure, layout, sections, and activities of pharmaceutical manufacturing plants
  • ▸ Quality control and quality assurance: definitions and core concepts
  • ▸ Current Good Manufacturing Practice (cGMP)
  • ▸ Introduction to calibration and validation
7

Novel Drug Delivery Systems

Chapter 7

  • ▸ Introduction and classification with examples
  • ▸ Advantages and challenges of novel drug delivery systems
