Theory Syllabus
Chapter 1
Introduction to pharmaceutical chemistry, sources and types of errors, and impurities in pharmaceuticals including limit test principles for chlorides, sulphates, iron, heavy metals, and arsenic.
Chapter 2
Volumetric analysis fundamentals: acid-base, non-aqueous, precipitation, complexometric, and redox titration. Gravimetric analysis: principle and method.
Chapter 3
Inorganic pharmaceuticals and their formulations, storage conditions, and uses: haematinics, GI agents, topical agents, dental products, and medicinal gases.
Chapter 4
Introduction to nomenclature of organic chemical systems with particular reference to heterocyclic compounds containing up to three rings.
Chapters 5 to 13
System-wise medicinal compounds with classification, chemical name, structure, uses, stability, storage conditions, formulations, and popular brand names: CNS drugs, autonomic nervous system drugs, cardiovascular drugs, diuretics, hypoglycemic agents, analgesic and anti-inflammatory agents, anti-infective agents, antibiotics, and anti-neoplastic agents.
