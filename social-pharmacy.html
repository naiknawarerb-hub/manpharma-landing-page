Theory Syllabus
Chapter 1
Introduction to Social Pharmacy including definition and scope, role of pharmacists in public health, concept of health, national health policy, public and private health system in India, National Health Mission, and development goals.
Chapter 2
Preventive healthcare and role of pharmacists in demography and family planning, mother and child health, breastfeeding, infant milk substitutes and bottle feeding, overview of vaccines and immunization, environmental health, and psychosocial pharmacy including misuse and abuse of drugs.
Chapter 3
Nutrition and health: basics of nutrition, water and fibres, balanced diet, malnutrition, deficiency diseases, junk food effects, calorific and nutritive values, food safety, adulteration, pesticides, GM foods, dietary supplements, nutraceuticals, and drug-food interactions.
Chapter 4
Introduction to microbiology and epidemiology, plus role of pharmacists in educating the public about communicable diseases including respiratory, intestinal, arthropod-borne, surface infections, and STDs/HIV/AIDS.
Chapter 5
Introduction to health systems and all ongoing national health programs in India, their objectives, functioning, outcomes, and role of pharmacists.
Chapter 6
Pharmacoeconomics: introduction, basic terminologies, and importance of pharmacoeconomics.
