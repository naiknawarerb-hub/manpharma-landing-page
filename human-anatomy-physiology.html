Theory Syllabus
Chapters 1 to 4
Scope of anatomy and physiology, basic terminologies, structure of cell and its functions, tissues of the human body, osseous system, and joints including classification, movements, and disorders.
Chapter 5
Haemopoietic system: composition and functions of blood, hemopoiesis, RBCs, WBCs and platelets, blood clotting, and blood groups.
Chapter 6
Lymphatic system: lymph and lymphatic system, composition, formation, spleen, and lymph node functions.
Chapter 7
Cardiovascular system: heart anatomy and physiology, circulation, cardiac cycle, heart sounds, basics of ECG, and blood pressure regulation.
Chapter 8
Respiratory system: anatomy of respiratory organs, regulation and mechanism of respiration, respiratory volumes and capacities.
Chapter 9
Digestive system: anatomy and physiology of the GIT, accessory glands, physiology of digestion and absorption.
Chapter 10
Skeletal muscles: histology, physiology of muscle contraction, and disorders of skeletal muscles.
Chapter 11
Nervous system: classification, cerebrum, cerebellum, mid brain, hypothalamus, medulla oblongata, basal ganglia, spinal cord, reflexes, cranial nerves, and ANS.
Chapter 12
Sense organs: anatomy and physiology of eye, ear, skin, tongue, and nose.
Chapter 13
Urinary system: anatomy and physiology, urine formation, renin-angiotensin system, clearance tests, and micturition.
Chapter 14
Endocrine system: pituitary gland, adrenal gland, thyroid and parathyroid gland, pancreas, and gonads.
Chapter 15
Reproductive system: male and female reproductive system, menstruation, spermatogenesis, oogenesis, pregnancy, and parturition.
